# Online Resource 5: Full results

Online Resource 5 to "Do Open-Source Video Ad Clients Follow VAST 4.3? An Empirical Study of Player Conformance" (Prashant Chaudhary, October 2026). This document holds the results that are too long for the paper: the requirement catalog with the obligation sentence behind every requirement, the full macro grid split by macro set, pointers to two generated views that list every verdict, once by client and once by duty group, with the oracle detail of each deviating cell, and the sensitivity of the must-level counts to strength labels for each configuration. Paths are relative to the root of the replication repository. Nothing here was rerun; each part is copied from the file named at its head, with section signs written as "Sec." and typographic quotation marks as plain ones. In the replication package this document is online-resources/OR5-full-results.md.

## 1. The requirement catalog with the obligation sentence behind every requirement

Source: generated from code/harness/suite/requirements.mjs, the VAST 4.3 text (sources/iab-vast-spec/VAST4.x/4.3.md) and the IAB macro list (sources/iab-vast-spec/vast/vast4macros/data/macros-data.json, commit 99d5d51); both are fetched and hash-checked by code/tools/fetch-sources.sh and are not stored in the replication package. Rows are sorted by ID in the order W, E, T, M, X, P, A, I, S, R, then by number. Appendix A of the paper gives the same requirements with their basis and literal modal; this table adds the sentence of the specification that carries each duty.

**What the catalog records.** Each entry has an ID, an area, a section reference, a strength, a duty and a one-line claim in the author's words. It records no sentence from the specification. Every obligation sentence below was therefore located in the VAST 4.3 text (or the macro list) for this table and is marked "located"; the generating script checks that each quoted fragment occurs verbatim on the cited line. "Where" is the line number in sources/iab-vast-spec/VAST4.x/4.3.md.

**Literal modal.** must, should or may as written; "required" where the text says "required" or "is required to" (treated as must); "imperative" for an instruction with no modal verb; "descriptive" for a definition or statement with no modal; "macro-list-required" where the only basis is the macro list's support label "Required"; "none" for robustness observations, which are not VAST clauses. Where several sentences are cited, the modal is the one that carries the graded duty (the note says which).

**Flag.** "differs" where the graded strength is not the literal modal (must against must or required; should against should; may against may; observation against none).

Catalog strengths: must 58, should 39, may 2, observation 9. Literal modals: must 32, required 22, should 22, descriptive 16, none 8, macro-list-required 5, may 2, imperative 1.
